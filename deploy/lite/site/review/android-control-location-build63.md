# Android APK 63 demonstration

`android-control-location-build63.mp4` is an edited, authentic ADB screen recording
of the production-signed APK in BlueStacks emulators, not a physical phone.
Duration: 139 seconds. Output: 1920 × 1080, H.264. English captions identify the
emulators and APK version. All scenes use one isolated demonstration family.

The recording shows an existing one-minute YouTube limit, enabled Usage access
and Display over other apps, persistent foreground-service notifications, actual
YouTube blocking, and blocking after dismissing the child app from Recents.
It also shows the parent location-sharing switch, the child's prominent
background-location disclosure, Android's permission request, the actual Always
permission state after manual approval, visible sharing status, the Android
location switch being disabled, the child's warning, and a real parent push.

Long waiting periods are cut. The owner's permission approval happened between
recordings; its click is not shown, and the video explicitly labels that cut.
No UI screens, notifications, permission states, or successful results were
fabricated. Calls/SMS applications are absent from these emulators and are not
demonstrated. No physical-device GPS fix or route is demonstrated.

The video is available directly for foreground-service review. It has not been
uploaded to YouTube. A background-location form requiring a YouTube URL remains
for the owner to complete after reviewing the demonstration.
