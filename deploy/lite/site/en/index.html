How it works

Three steps, two phones

  1. The parent sets tasks

    The class timetable, homework per subject and chores: pack the school bag, read for 20 minutes.

  2. The child does them

    Checks tasks off on their own phone and attaches a photo of the finished work — camera only, per subject.

  3. The parent sees it

    What was done and when, photos, requests to skip a task. Approve, comment or send a reminder with one tap.

Features

Everything for the school day in one place

Screen time Android

Rules for your child’s phone, set up on your own phone:

  • a daily limit and limits for individual apps;
  • bedtime and school hours — only allowed apps;
  • “Homework first”: in the evening games stay locked until the required tasks are done;
  • new apps only with your approval;
  • “Give more time” with one tap.

Calls, messages and emergency calls always work. The child sees a notification that parental control is on: no hidden monitoring, messages and app content are never read. On iPhone the rules are applied through Apple’s Screen Time — the child must be in your Family Sharing group.

Homework and to-dos

Homework from the timetable and tasks for the day. A task can be skipped only with a parent’s approval.

Photos of finished work

Snap the exercise book right in the app. Photos are kept 7–40 days — you choose.

Reminders

For the child — ahead of time; for the parent — if a task isn’t done on time. Quiet hours so nobody is woken up.

Chat and voice notes

A question about a problem goes into the chat right on the task. The child can record a voice note.

Calendar and reports

The month in colours: on time, late, not done. Streaks and stars for effort.

Textbooks

Links to e-textbooks and your own PDFs. They open inside the app, even when games are locked.

Invite by QR code

The child, a second parent or a tutor joins with a code or QR — no passwords, no email.

What it looks like

A calm design, like a school exercise book
